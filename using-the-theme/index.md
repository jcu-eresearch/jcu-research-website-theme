Create a website that explains your research clearly. You can begin with a simple text page and add richer layouts as you need them. You do not need to know GitHub, Markdown, or YAML before starting these guides.

Each area has its own menu and overview page. Detailed pages live within that section, and their breadcrumbs link back to its overview.

## New to this? Start here

[Beginners: start here]({{ "/beginners/" | relative_url }}) explains how the website works, how to get it online, and how to write your first page using small, copyable examples.

## Build your pages

[Build your pages]({{ "/build-your-pages/" | relative_url }}) helps you choose a standard page or landing page, understand content blocks, and combine writing methods.

There are two general-purpose page layouts: a **standard page** for detailed information and a **landing page** for an overview that guides visitors to more detail. Plain Markdown, YAML blocks, Markdown with layout classes, and HTML wrappers with Markdown are compatible authoring methods. They can be mixed on the same page; they are not four separate page layouts.

## Look up a task

[Content and styling reference]({{ "/reference/" | relative_url }}) links to Markdown and YAML basics, block recipes, card collections, appearance settings, navigation, and detailed syntax guides.

## Check and maintain

[Check and maintain]({{ "/check-and-maintain/" | relative_url }}) covers accessibility, colour choices, previewing, troubleshooting, and keeping the website useful as your research develops.

Explore the [Sample content styles]({{ "/sample-content/" | relative_url }}) to see working examples of the theme's content patterns.
