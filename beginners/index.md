You can create a research website without learning to program. Start with one useful page, then add more as your project develops. These guides explain the unfamiliar words as they arise; you can edit your files in GitHub's browser editor without installing development tools.

## Your first website

1. [What this theme does]({{ "/beginners/what-this-theme-does/" | relative_url }}) — Understand websites, themes, and the files you will edit.
2. [How your website gets online]({{ "/beginners/how-your-website-gets-online/" | relative_url }}) — Meet GitHub, repositories, commits, and GitHub Pages.
3. [Create your first website]({{ "/beginners/create-your-first-website/" | relative_url }}) — Set up a project site and publish a small homepage.
4. [Write your first page]({{ "/beginners/write-your-first-page/" | relative_url }}) — Copy a simple page, write your text, and add it to the menu.

## What to learn next

You only need a little [Markdown]({{ "/reference/plain-markdown/" | relative_url }}) and [YAML]({{ "/reference/yaml-basics/" | relative_url }}) to begin. A page can remain a straightforward document. When you want to group related information into panels, columns, or cards, continue to [Build your pages]({{ "/build-your-pages/" | relative_url }}).

Before sharing your site, use [Check and maintain]({{ "/check-and-maintain/" | relative_url }}).
